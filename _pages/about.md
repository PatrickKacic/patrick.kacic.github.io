As a scientist, I enjoy analyzing environmental datasets to study ecosystem dynamics, such as disturbances, structural changes, and biodiversity responses to management. 
With a focus on time-series observations from various Earth Observation sensors, I am fascinated by the perspective from space to monitor natural and anthropogenic drivers of environmental change in the context of the global climate crisis. :earth_africa:

Outdoors in my freetime, but also during work-related field campaigns, and conference excursions, I specifically appreciate exploring forest ecosystems. :deciduous_tree:

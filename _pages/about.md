As a scientist, I enjoy analyzing environmental datasets to study ecosystem dynamics. With a focus on time-series observations from various Earth Observation (EO) sensors, 
I am fascinated by the perspective from space to monitor natural and anthropogenic drivers of environmental change in the context of the global climate crisis. :earth_africa:

I am currently employed at the German Aerospace Center (DLR) as a postdoctoral researcher in the team [Forest Ecosystems](https://www.dlr.de/en/eoc/about-us/german-remote-sensing-data-center/land-surface-dynamics/forest-ecosystems) 
assessing structural changes, disturbances, and biodiversity responses to management in European forest ecosystems.
In the [EO4CAM project](https://www.dlr.de/en/site/eo4cam), we develop [EO-based datasets](https://eo4cam.dlr.de/#/home) for climate adaptation and mitigation in Bavaria, Germany. 
In former projects, I contributed to research on forest structure-biodiversity ([BETA-FOR](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/en/beta-for/)) relationships in the context of management, 
deforestation monitoring ([Geo-ForPy](https://www.dlr.de/en/eoc/research-transfer/projects-missions/geo-forpy)),
and monthly ([ForstEO](https://www.dlr.de/en/eoc/research-transfer/projects-missions/forsteo)), as well as near-real time forest disturbance mapping ([ROOT](https://www.dlr.de/en/eoc/research-transfer/projects-missions/joint-project-r)). :artificial_satellite:

Outdoors in my freetime, but also during work-related field campaigns, and conference excursions, I specifically appreciate exploring forest ecosystems. :deciduous_tree:
